## Strong relationships surfaced by descriptions

- **Ain't No Sunshine** directly points to **The Mountain Remembers**, **In Her 20-s**, and **EWP Project** as other experimental audiovisual works.
- **The Mountain Remembers** connects to Mount Fuji, Fuji Five Lakes, Aokigahara, Lake Saiko, UTAU voicebanks, Julia Baranyuk, field recordings, and the JIHANKI/Japan research branch.
- **二十歳 (In Her 20-s)** is an original UTAU / experimental video-art work featuring Akane Iirai.
- **子供の空 (Child's Sky)** connects Kasane Teto and Akane Iirai within the audiovisual/UTAU branch.
- **しあわせがこわい** connects the audio/video archive with the 友人 (Eugene) voicebank.
- **Get Hit Below**, **Fukkireta**, **Dymohod**, **Mudak**, **Sergaj**, **Lupen**, **Pjesma pro Ljubov** and related geztålt uploads form a dense music-video cluster around the geztålt project.
- **Taming the Serpent** connects the video archive to a 2022 collaborative performance with Julia Baranyuk at Winzavod.
- **Agonal Machines** connects the video archive to live performance documentation in Moscow, including geztålt and other performers.
- **EWP Project** exists both as the 2023 original cut and a later festival-cut upload; retain both as records linked to one work.

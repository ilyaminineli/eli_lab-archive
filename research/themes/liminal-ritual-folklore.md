# Theme: liminal / ritual / constructed folklore

## Cluster
- Metaliminal / Sleepdom / Desolation Odyssey / Elysium — liminal game-language family, currently partly unresolved.
- Found Tape — fictional found-media and curse logic.
- Баронъ / Бессоница — digital folklore / experimental film.
- Ritual for the Big Refrigerator / Evocation Turtur — domestic or absurd ritual transformed into performance/video.
- Geztålt — cultural decomposition, imitation, simulation, wordplay.
- JIHANKI — ordinary infrastructure transformed into a machine/ritual interface.
- Seven Hills M7 — fictional microcassette archive and synthetic voice.
- WHO IS ELI? — archive/reconstruction/horror system.

## Shared mechanics
ordinary object → fictional system → archive/document → altered reality

## Why this matters
This theme connects otherwise distant games, sound works, video experiments and archive interfaces. It is a better cross-medium index than keeping everything under an isolated 'dark/experimental' label.

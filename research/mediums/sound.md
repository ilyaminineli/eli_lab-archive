# Sound / music / sound art

## Historical records
### Epäorgaanisten Rakenteiden Kierrätys
2022. Semi-hermetic autobiographical narrative; electroacoustic / avant-garde orientation. Ilya Minin: sound programming and edit. Romaner: design and cover.

### Lab Announcement 3
2023. Sparse analogue instrumentation, processed field recordings, microchromatics and granular synthesis; described through electroacoustics, dance music and computational formulas. Ilya: sound programming and edit. Romaner: cover/design.

### Geztålt
Collaborative project focused on decomposing cultural forms through wordplay, sound performance, media content and theoretical concepts from anthropology, semantics, epistemology and media theory. The old site also contains an interview and event records.

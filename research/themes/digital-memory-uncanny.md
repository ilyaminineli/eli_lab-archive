# Theme: digital memory / uncanny

## Cluster
- EWP Project — altered hometown, memory, identity, belonging.
- Daly Syndrome — sound, perception, memory, derealization, audiovirus premise.
- The Last Day of Kasane Teto — virtual identity and digital melancholy.
- Triple Baka — autobiography filtered through Vocaloid culture.
- In Her 20-s — synthetic voice, medical/disappearance imagery.
- Child's Sky — childhood memory and surreal landscape.
- Someone's Voice — unstable inner voice / reflection.
- I'm Afraid of Happiness — fear, vulnerability, happiness and unease.
- Found Tape — fictional found-media logic.
- REBIRTH, W, Barонъ, Бессоница and other older video works use similar unstable or mediated-reality strategies.

## Recurring formal devices
archive fragments, synthetic voices, degraded/alternate media, collage, loops, fictional documentation, digital folklore, constructed identities.

## Relation to systems
The uncanny theme is often implemented through a system: procedural animation, synthesized voice, algorithmic sound, archive interfaces, deterministic graphics or found-media structures.

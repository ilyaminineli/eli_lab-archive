# Visual / physical practice

## Source families
- Painting — Head 1, Head 2, Head 3 (2022), oil on canvas.
- Graphics — A gift to a Jewish fund, four sheets (2022), pencil, capillary pen, felt-tip pens, crayons.
- Mixed Technique — Study 1–3 and Anthology of Black Humor 1–7 (2022), photo collage / manual processing.
- Installation — No Skin (2022), plastic bust, lamp, colored paper.
- Photography — Portraits, Unreal, Serial Experiments Lens, Single Photos.
- CGI / 3D — 3D Essentials, Semi-spatial Apartment, Bloody/Bloody 2, Bonza, broader CG design archive.

## Serial Experiments Lens
The 2021–2023 series bridges photography, collage, optics and CGI. Set 1 contains windowshots, watershots, digital and analogue collage, macro work, binocular-as-lens studies, CGI and later digital photographs.

## Duplicate hazards
- CGI exists as both project taxonomy and art-practice taxonomy.
- 3D Essentials is a series, not several unrelated projects.
- Parallel Vienna is an event/design record; do not use a generic painting thumbnail as proof of that work.
